For testers

Thank you for testing Turnstone.

Turnstone shows how someone works through four short workplace situations and three reasoning tests. You'll sit it as a candidate would, and tell us what worked and what didn't.

What to do

  1. Join with your invitation link. It works only with the email address it was sent to, once.
  2. Answer a few questions about you. Your background and what you're looking for. Use made-up answers if you prefer: this is a test.
  3. Try the practice room. About five minutes, not scored.
  4. Work through the four situations, in order, and the three reasoning tests, at any time. Each situation is designed to take 15 to 20 minutes, and you can stop and come back.
  5. Write the short note, check your words and send your profile, then read your full profile.
  6. Tell us what you think. Use "Tell us what you think" on your profile page, inside Turnstone, at any point.

Worth noticing as you go

Was anything unclear, slow or fiddly? Did the situations feel real? Did your reports tell you something useful about how you work, and was it fair? Would you take it seriously if an employer asked you to do it? Notes like these help most.
