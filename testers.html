01 / What is Pathfinder

Pathfinder is a defence career intelligence platform. It uses visual scenario assessments that measure how you think rather than what you know: you study an operational scene and respond across four phases, Observe, Assess, Recommend and Anticipate.

Alongside the scenarios there are psychometric reasoning modules covering numerical, verbal and logical reasoning. Each assessment produces a detailed report.

03 / What to pay attention to

Worth noticing as you go.

Did the scenario feel realistic and engaging?

Was the image interesting to analyse? Did you find yourself looking deeper?

Were the phase prompts clear?

How did the report feel? Fair? Surprising?

Any rough edges, confusing moments, or things that felt off?

Overall, would you take this seriously as part of a hiring process?
