The gap

The CV, the tests and the interview each show something. None shows how someone works a messy situation.

Turnstone is built around the hardest part of a senior move: getting things done through people who don't report to you. In every situation the candidate arrives new, with no one to give orders to: a founder, investors, a partner, a team they have only just met.

How it works

Four situations, three tests, one profile.

The same for every candidate, so you are comparing like with like.

  1. A short practice room

    About five minutes, with hints, so nobody is caught out by the format. It is never scored.

  2. Four workplace situations

    Each is one picture and a short brief: a payroll incident, a workshop floor, an investor review, a partner meeting. The candidate pins what matters, joins the things that belong together, makes the calls, responds when news arrives, chooses who to approach first, and says what would change their mind. Each is designed to take 15 to 20 minutes.

  3. Three reasoning tests

    Numerical, verbal and logical, taken at any time. Shown beside the situations, never added to them.

  4. A short written note

    About 80 words, shown to you exactly as written. Never scored.

  5. The candidate checks and sends

    They check the words their profile quotes, then send it. Only then do you see it, so every profile you see is complete.

What you get

A profile you can read in minutes, and use in the interview.

How they work, situation by situation

What they approached first, what their links showed and what else the situation held, what they decided, and what they changed when the news came. Their strengths and what to probe, with their own words where they wrote them.

An interview guide

Questions drawn from what they actually did, to explore in the interview. They name the situation, never the answer.

Side by side

Two or three candidates together, in the order you choose, with their reasoning tests charted. Never sorted, never totalled.

Share with your clients

Invite your clients' people. They see only the candidates you share with them, once each profile is ready, and can compare them side by side.

Why it's different

Fair to the candidate, and explainable to you.

What they did, not what they say

The profile is built from their choices in each situation, not from how they describe themselves. It describes how they worked, never what kind of person they are.

Fixed rules, no AI marking

Every candidate is scored by the same rules, written in advance. Every mark traces back to something they did, so a result can be explained and checked. No AI marks anyone.

Never ranked

No single score, no league table, no pass mark. Turnstone is one more way to see a candidate, beside everything else you know.

The candidate gets a report too

They see their own profile, telling the same story you read, with language they can use about how they work.

Background as context only

Candidates can tell you about their background and what they are looking for. It is shown as context and never scored. Clearance is shown only if they choose.

Invitation only

Candidates join by your invitation. Nothing is public, and each client sees only what you share with them.
